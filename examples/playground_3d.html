Reading the two layers

3D scene = truth (the witness channel): drones, trails, sensor volumes at real ranges. PPI scope = measurements (what the sensors actually report): green radar hits, amber clutter, cyan EO, fuchsia RF rim arcs (RF never gives range), dashed ghosts = truth. The gap between the two layers is the entire research problem.

⚠ The playground needs its Python server

then open http://localhost:8000/examples/playground_3d.html
(the command above also opens it for you automatically).
